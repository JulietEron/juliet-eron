Bespoke Services

Bespoke Services

Every Juliet Eron garment is individually designed and handcrafted, from your first idea to the finished piece. This is our primary craft.

How It Works

Your Vision, Realised

You're welcome to bring your own design ideas, inspiration photographs or your own fabric — we'll work from what you already have in mind. If you're starting from scratch, we'll guide you through choosing a fabric and silhouette suited to your occasion.

Either way, every garment is created through collaboration — a series of conversations and fittings, not a finished decision handed to you.

What's Included

Custom Designed Garments, Start to Finish

Personal Consultation

A one-on-one conversation about your occasion and vision.

Custom Design

Your garment is designed specifically for you, not adapted from stock.

Fabric Guidance

Bring your own fabric, or receive guidance choosing the right one.

Fittings Until Perfect

We refine the fit through fittings until it's exactly right.
